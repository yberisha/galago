Ticket Username Sync Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Ticket Username Sync reads ticket and engineer information from the 3CIS task dashboard and related CMS page to match records and display information in the task table. It stores mappings locally in your browser using Chrome storage. CSV export is optional and started by the user.

The extension uses your logged-in 3CIS and CMS pages to retrieve related information. It can submit a 3CIS pre-check when the applicable checks pass. The extension developer does not receive this information through a separate server or analytics service.

You can remove locally stored mappings by clearing the extension’s data in Chrome or uninstalling the extension.
