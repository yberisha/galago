Ticket Username Sync Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Information the extension handles

Ticket Username Sync reads information from the 3CIS task dashboard and the CMS Code Upgrade Dashboard that is needed to match tickets and display related information. This may include ticket identifiers, engineer names or usernames, and relevant task or CMS fields such as status and related ticket details. This is website content and may include personally identifiable information, such as a person's name or username.

How information is used and stored

The extension uses this information to match tickets, display related CMS information in the task table, and maintain ticket-to-engineer mappings. Mappings are stored locally in your browser using Chrome storage so they remain available across browser sessions. The extension does not send this information to a developer-operated server or analytics service.

Communication with 3CIS and the CMS

The extension works with the 3CIS and CMS pages you are signed in to, and information requests are made to those services to provide the extension's features. When the applicable status and data checks pass, the extension can submit the 3CIS pre-check to 3CIS.

CSV export and deletion

CSV export is optional and is started by you. You choose where to save or share the exported file. You can remove locally stored mappings by clearing the extension's data in Chrome or uninstalling the extension.
